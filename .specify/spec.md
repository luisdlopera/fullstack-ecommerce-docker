# SellFlow System — Product Specification

## Problem Statement

Millions of small businesses in Latin America sell through WhatsApp daily, but they face critical operational challenges:

- **Disorganized orders**: Customer messages get lost in chat history, leading to missed sales and frustrated customers
- **No inventory control**: Selling products they don't have or missing restock opportunities
- **No order tracking**: Customers repeatedly asking "where is my order?" without a clear answer
- **Manual customer management**: No history of previous purchases or customer preferences
- **Scattered information**: Data spread across notebooks, spreadsheets, and chat apps

These businesses lose money, customers, and growth opportunities simply because they lack a system designed for their reality.

## Solution: SellFlow System

**SellFlow** is an order management system specifically designed for businesses that sell through WhatsApp. Unlike generic e-commerce platforms, it embraces the reality of conversational commerce while adding structure, control, and professionalism.

### Value Proposition

> **"From chaos to control: manage your WhatsApp sales like a professional operation without changing how your customers buy."**

---

## Core Features

### 1. Order Management

- **Order lifecycle**: Draft → Confirmed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered/Cancelled
- **WhatsApp-optimized flow**: Capture orders from chat conversations
- **Order notes**: Internal comments and customer communication history
- **Status notifications**: Automatic updates to customers
- **Order search & filter**: Find orders by customer, date, status, or product

### 2. Inventory Control

- **Real-time stock tracking**: Accurate inventory across all products
- **Multi-location support**: Manage stock across multiple warehouses or stores
- **Low stock alerts**: Automatic notifications when inventory runs low
- **Stock movements**: Complete audit trail of every inventory change
- **Product catalog**: Organized by categories with images and descriptions

### 3. Customer Management

- **Customer profiles**: Contact info, purchase history, preferences
- **Order history**: Complete view of all customer transactions
- **Customer segmentation**: Tag customers by type, frequency, or value
- **Quick reorder**: Easy reordering based on purchase history
- **WhatsApp integration**: Direct link to customer chat

### 4. WhatsApp-First Design

- **Order from chat**: Convert WhatsApp conversations into system orders
- **Quick product catalog**: Shareable product list for WhatsApp
- **Status updates**: Notify customers directly about order progress
- **Mobile-optimized**: Full functionality on mobile devices
- **No customer app needed**: Customers continue using WhatsApp normally

### 5. Admin Panel

- **Dashboard overview**: Orders, revenue, inventory status at a glance
- **Role-based access**: Admin, manager, and operator permissions
- **Reports & analytics**: Sales trends, top products, customer insights
- **User management**: Add team members with specific permissions

## Key Differentiators

### NOT a Generic E-Commerce

| Generic E-Commerce | SellFlow |
|-------------------|------------|
| Customers browse a website | Orders come from WhatsApp conversations |
| Automated checkout | Manual order confirmation workflow |
| Payment gateway integration | Cash/transfer payments tracked manually |
| Shipping integrations | Local delivery management |
| Anonymous buyers | Known customers with history |

### Built for WhatsApp Commerce

- **Conversational orders**: System designed around chat-based sales
- **Human-in-the-loop**: Orders confirmed by business owner, not automated
- **Flexible payments**: Supports cash, bank transfers, mobile payments
- **Local delivery**: Optimized for in-house or local courier delivery
- **Relationship-focused**: Built to strengthen customer relationships

## Non-Goals (Phase 1)

These features are intentionally excluded from initial scope:

- **AI chatbots**: No automated customer conversation handling
- **Payment processing**: No integrated payment gateways (manual tracking only)
- **Marketplace features**: No multi-vendor support
- **Advanced analytics**: Basic reports only; no predictive analytics
- **Mobile app**: Web-based responsive design only
